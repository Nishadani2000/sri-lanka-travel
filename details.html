Destination Guides & Travel Details

Riverston


Best Time to Visit - January to April and July to August offer the clearest skies.

Optimal Time of Day - Before 8:00 AM to beat the thick, rolling valley mists.

Trek Difficulty - Easy to Moderate (mostly paved roads or clear forest pathways).

Road Conditions - Narrow with sharp hairpin turns; passable but requires careful driving.

What to Pack - Sturdy walking shoes, rain jacket, leach socks, and plenty of water.


Ella


Best Time to Visit: - The driest and clearest weather occurs between January and March.

Vibe: The main street is packed with lively backpacker cafes, boutique shops, and restaurants like the famous Cafe Chill.

Packing: Bring lightweight layers, as the days can be warm and sunny while evenings get surprisingly chilly.

Ohiya


How to get there

By Train: The most scenic option is taking the Main Line train (e.g., Podi Menike or Udarata Menike) from Colombo or Kandy and getting off directly at the colonial-era Ohiya Railway Station.

By Road: Accessible via Boralanda or the steep mountain routes from Welimada.

Climate & VibeWeather: Crisp and refreshing, with daytime temperatures between 15°C and 20°C, dropping to around 5°C at night. Heavy fog and thick mist are very common during the mornings and late afternoons.

Atmosphere: Unlike bustling tourist hubs like Ella or Nuwara Eliya, Ohiya maintains a completely rustic, untouched rural charm with minimal commercial crowds.

Ambuluwawa


Opening Hours: - Open daily from 7:30 AM to 5:00 PM.

Best Time to Visit: - Arriving by 8:30 AM during the dry season (December to March). Avoid visiting during rainy conditions as the external stairs become dangerous and slick.

Ticket Prices - Entry fees for international tourists stand at LKR 2,750 for adults and LKR 1,500 for children.

Belwood


Distance from Kandy - Approximately 18 to 20 kilometers southwest of Kandy city.

How to Get There - You can reach Bellwood from Kandy in about an hour by taking a local Galaha bus, or by driving a tuk-tuk/car or bike via Ampitiya and the scenic Moragolla Road.
